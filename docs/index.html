Verto

AI Innovation Pipeline

A business-level common ground document for an AI tool that refines raw employee ideas into R&D-ready opportunities.

Thesis Actors Campaigns Idea flow Research Knowledge system

Business Thesis

Innovation teams already receive more ideas than they can evaluate. The product focuses on quality under overload: refining vague submissions, preserving meaningful variations, and routing only decision-ready opportunities into active review.

Main promise: always-open idea capture plus campaign-specific refinement, without optimizing for raw submission volume, inventing R&D strategy, or collapsing distinct idea variants too early.

Verto's center of gravity is idea intake and refinement. Campaign setup supports that flow, and Global Innovation Context improves it over time.

Actors

Employee

Submits raw ideas through the portal. May receive an emailed clarification link when an idea needs more detail. Their identity is visible to R&D for collaboration after selection, but should not drive AI evaluation.

R&D Team

Owns campaigns, decides what topics employees see, reviews refined ideas, and marks true out-of-scope patterns. The R&D leader is the economic buyer; the R&D Campaign Owner, also called an R&D Team Lead, is the champion for a campaign. R&D Team Members can contribute draft answers, but owner approval is required before those answers become campaign ground truth.

AI Agent

Interviews R&D for campaign common ground, asks clarifying questions, organizes ideas, and cites documented rules when evaluating submissions.

Manager collaboration matters after selection in the real business workflow, but it is not a required MVP demo actor.

Campaign Model

A campaign is an R&D-owned innovation theme. It may be lightweight, incomplete, intentionally vague, evolving, or fully detailed when the business need requires precision. The AI should help R&D decide which parts should stay broad and which parts need explicit detail.

Active Campaign

A current R&D request or theme. Ideas mapped to active campaigns receive higher triage priority.

Always-Open Campaign

A long-term, indefinite campaign for general ideas. It keeps the portal open even when no specific campaign fits.

Principle

R&D chooses employee-facing topics. AI can suggest wording and identify gaps, but it should not decide what themes employees are invited to submit against.

R&D Campaign Interview

Before ideas are judged, AI opens a structured discussion with R&D using question mode. The goal is not to force narrow rules. The goal is shared understanding.

  1. R&D creates or updates a campaign.
  2. AI asks one targeted question at a time and recommends a concrete answer.
  3. AI asks what should be broad, explicit, hidden, or employee-facing.
  4. AI explores unknown unknowns with R&D and records resolved decisions.
  5. If R&D lacks enough context, AI asks for relevant track records and synthesizes patterns.
  6. R&D defines the campaign-specific minimum review packet.
  7. R&D confirms intentional ambiguity where flexibility matters.
  8. AI records campaign ground truth for later idea evaluation.
  9. Future AI judgments cite the campaign docs.
Campaign setup is AI-first: R&D works through a global agent question queue, the R&D Campaign Owner clears the owner attention queue, then reviews the campaign-specific HTML knowledge report before opening intake.

Hard blockers prevent opening intake. Warnings and skipped clarity questions can remain visible in the knowledge report without blocking launch.

Campaign Setup Knowledge

Each campaign produces a campaign-specific HTML knowledge report that describes what AI understands and will follow for that campaign. R&D does not edit the report directly; corrections go back through AI conversation so the report remains generated from setup state.

If Company Context or Global Innovation Context conflicts with campaign intent, the R&D Campaign Owner can approve a campaign-only override. The override is recorded in the Campaign Knowledge Report, but only the R&D Manager can change the published Company Context or Global Innovation Context.

AI Setup

AI uses question mode to ask blockers, warnings, and clarity questions across the whole campaign setup, and follows up with the R&D Campaign Owner when owner judgment is unclear.

Structured View

R&D can inspect setup fields in a form-like view, but stored changes still go through AI interpretation and checking.

Idea Flow

  1. Submit Employee submits an idea to a visible campaign or the always-open campaign.
  2. Clarify AI emails a clarification conversation link when the idea does not satisfy the campaign-specific minimum review packet. The conversation is preloaded with relevant campaign docs and evaluation context.
  3. Compare AI finds related ideas but preserves meaningful variants instead of merging by surface similarity.
  4. Screen AI checks documented R&D-out-of-scope patterns and can stop employee interview early when a clear match exists.
  5. Route Ideas become campaign-fit, adjacent, parked, needs-detail, duplicate-candidate, or R&D-out-of-scope.
  6. Review R&D receives a refined packet with cited reasoning, open questions, and variant context.

Similarity And Variants

Similar core intent does not mean duplicate idea. Vegan menu ideas that use mushroom, tofu, legumes, or plant-based meat may differ in cost, preparation, nutrition, allergens, supply chain, customer appeal, and brand positioning.

AI should tag related ideas as variant candidates and let R&D decide whether they compete, combine, or deserve separate review.

Pre-R&D Classification

All idea interviews and classification happen before presenting the idea to R&D. The AI should classify the idea, connect it to related ideas, identify variants, and cite campaign or out-of-scope docs before R&D spends review time.

If an idea clearly matches a documented R&D-out-of-scope pattern, AI can stop asking the employee for more detail and classify it accordingly.

Employee Clarification Visibility

Employee clarification should show the submitted idea, public campaign context, focused AI questions, and optional examples of useful detail. It should hide internal R&D criteria, scoring, ranking, related employee ideas, sensitive out-of-scope rule names, campaign strategy notes, and Global Innovation Context.

Campaign Learning Loop

The system suggests potential ideas, but R&D chooses what to review, prototype, commercialize, park, reject, or mark out of scope. When R&D cannot define campaign criteria up front, AI should ask for track records from prior campaigns and synthesize them into a working synopsis.

End-of-campaign decisions are not only outcomes. AI can run a post-campaign question-mode interview with R&D to understand what happened and improve future campaign common ground.

Learnings can stay campaign-specific, become Global Innovation Context, or both. AI may recommend the scope, but R&D chooses how broadly each learning applies. Campaign-specific exceptions should usually become global proposals during the post-campaign retrospective, not during live campaign setup.

When Global Innovation Context conflicts with campaign intent, AI should raise the conflict during the R&D interview and ask which context governs the campaign.

Company Context

Company context is optional reference context for campaign setup. R&D Managers can create it from public web sources, manual descriptions, and uploaded files. AI turns that input into an HTML report, asks conflict or clarity questions when needed, and the R&D Manager approves the report before it becomes usable context.

Company context informs campaign setup but does not overwrite campaign decisions. When it conflicts with a campaign, AI asks R&D to resolve the conflict inside that campaign's setup flow.

If Company Context is not set up, company reference context is empty. General global idea-intake rules live in the always-open campaign.

When Company Context or Global Innovation Context changes, active campaign owners may receive non-blocking context change alerts. These alerts help owners review possible impact, but they do not block intake or overwrite Campaign Context.

Demo Story

The hackathon demo should show the process truthfully instead of forcing fixed counts or outcomes. It should demonstrate campaign setup, R&D interview, employee idea submission, clarification when needed, variant detection, out-of-scope handling when applicable, pre-R&D classification, R&D selection authority, and post-campaign learning.

The core wow moment is AI turning vague R&D intent and messy employee submissions into a documented, explainable candidate set R&D can act on.

MVP fidelity: use real email sending for clarification links, preferably Pingram. Historical track records can be scripted if useful. Company directory data and commercialization outcomes can be simulated.

Demo clarification links may use secure magic links without login. Real enterprise deployments should use company identity controls such as SSO, employee directory permissions, link expiry, and audit logging.

Knowledge System

The knowledge system should preserve relationships between campaigns, ideas, decisions, ambiguity, and out-of-scope rules. The final R&D-facing format is still open and may become an HTML view, dashboard, structured report, cards, graph, or mixed interface.
