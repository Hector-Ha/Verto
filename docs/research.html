Why R&D Gets Overloaded

Open innovation and employee idea programs can produce far more input than teams can evaluate. The bottleneck moves from idea collection to screening, clarification, prioritization, and implementation.

Verto should position itself after engagement succeeds: the company already has many ideas, but R&D needs better quality, context, and routing before review.

Open Innovation Precedent

My Starbucks Idea is a strong reference case. Starbucks reported more than 150,000 submitted ideas and 277 implemented innovations within five years. Public summaries describe examples such as free Wi-Fi, cake pops, mobile payments, splash sticks, and rewards-related ideas.

The important lesson is not only participation. It is the operating burden: ideas needed reading, filtering, feedback, escalation to decision makers, and implementation follow-through.

Product Implications

  1. Collection is solved High-volume programs show people will submit ideas when the company creates a visible channel.
  2. Screening becomes scarce R&D and innovation teams need help comparing, clarifying, clustering, and routing ideas.
  3. Feedback matters Visible status and clear rationale prevent the portal from feeling like a black box.
  4. Variation matters Similarity detection must preserve meaningful variants instead of treating all related ideas as duplicates.
